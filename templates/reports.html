Report Contents

Each generated AttackLens report is designed to summarize the complete security analysis workflow.

01
Executive Summary

High-level overview of the analyzed environment, overall risk posture, and important findings.

02
Asset Inventory

Discovered assets, operating systems, exposed ports, services, and asset context.

03
Vulnerability & Risk Assessment

Identified vulnerabilities, CVE information, asset risk scores, and risk classifications.

04
Attack Path Analysis

Potential attacker progression paths and evidence-supported graph relationships.

05
Defense Analysis

Security findings, defensive priorities, and identified defensive focus areas.

06
Mitigation Recommendations

Evidence-based defensive actions and expected defensive effects.

07
Before vs After Risk Comparison

Current security state compared with the projected post-mitigation state.

08
Methodology & Disclaimer

Explanation of analytical assumptions, evidence limitations, and report scope.
