Analysis Context

How AttackLens interprets attack-path results

◫

Potential Security Relationships

AttackLens generates potential attack paths from available evidence such as exposed services, mapped vulnerabilities, security findings, asset exposure, risk, criticality, and explicitly supported asset relationships. A vulnerability or open port alone does not prove an attack path. Results represent potential attacker progression and do not prove successful exploitation, compromise, network reachability, or lateral movement.
