How Mitigation Works

Evidence-based defensive guidance, not automatic remediation.

✓

Evidence-Based Mitigation

AttackLens generates recommendations only when current scan, asset, vulnerability, exposure, defense, relationship, or attack-path evidence supports them. Recommendations describe defensive actions for review and do not automatically modify the analyzed systems.

Recommendation priority scores are relative defensive prioritization indicators. A high score does not establish confirmed exploitation, guaranteed reachability, or guaranteed risk reduction.
